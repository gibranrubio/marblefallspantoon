FAQ

Common Questions

Is gas included?
Yes. Gas is included in the rental price. No hidden fuel costs.

Are life vests included?
Yes. Life vests are provided for all passengers.

Can we bring food and drinks?
Yes. Bring snacks, drinks, towels, and anything you need for a great lake day.

Do we need boating experience?
No experience needed. We will walk you through everything before your rental. The renter must be responsible and follow all lake rules.

What is the hourly minimum?
A 3-hour minimum is recommended for the best experience.

Is a deposit required?
A deposit may be required to reserve your date. Details confirmed at booking.

What is the cancellation policy?
Cancellations and weather reschedules are handled case-by-case. The goal is to keep things simple and fair.

Where is the launch point?
Rentals are on Lake Marble Falls. Exact launch and meet-up details are confirmed when you book.
