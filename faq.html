Common Questions

Where are rentals located?
Rentals are on Lake Marble Falls in Marble Falls, TX. Exact launch point and meet-up details are confirmed when you book.

How long is a rental?
A 3-hour minimum is recommended for the best experience. Longer blocks are available for half-day or full-day outings — call for pricing.

What is required to make a reservation?
The reserving person should have a valid ID and credit card. A deposit may be required to lock in the reservation.

Is a captain available?
Yes, a captain can be available upon request depending on date, time, and availability.

Is gas included?
Yes. Gas is included in the rental price. No hidden fuel costs.

Are life vests included?
Yes. Life vests are provided for all passengers.

Can we bring food and drinks?
Yes. Bring snacks, drinks, towels, and anything you need for a great lake day.

Do we need boating experience?
No experience needed. We will walk you through everything before your rental. The renter must be responsible and follow all lake rules.

What should we bring?
Towels, sunscreen, drinks, snacks, and anything you need for a comfortable lake day. Life jackets and basic safety gear are provided on the boat.

Are there discounted rates?
Yes — discounted rates are available for multiple hours. Call us at 737-202-5367 for half and full day pricing.

What is the cancellation policy?
Cancellations and weather reschedules are handled case-by-case. The goal is to keep things simple and fair for everyone.

What if the weather looks bad?
Forecasted rain does not always mean a rental has to cancel. If weather creates unsafe lake conditions at the time of the outing, rescheduling can be discussed.
